# ResearchMate AI v0.5.0
Delivered: multi-select article transfer, local paper/project save and CRUD, future-year validation, output-type selection, weighted 100-point rubric, bounded iterative revision prompt, scientific writing rules, icons and transitions.
Boundaries: projects use app-private SharedPreferences JSON and may be lost on app-data clear/uninstall. Citation counts are not journal quality. Rubric score is not journal quartile. The app generates the prompt but does not run an LLM or verify the final score.
Next: 0.6 Room database/tags/notes/backup/PDF picker; 0.7 legal full-text retrieval/PDF parsing/OCR/evidence anchors; 0.8 AI provider adapters/evidence-linked synthesis/revision and quotas; 0.9 PDF/DOCX export, bibliography verification, signed APK and device/privacy tests.
